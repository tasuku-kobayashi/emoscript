emoscript browser edition

Paste candidate taglines, one per line. Each is embedded in your browser and scored against the direction of famous ad copy. Your lines are never sent anywhere. The page loads its code from cdn.jsdelivr.net and the embedding model once from huggingface.co (cached by the browser).

p is the calibrated probability that a line sits with famous copy rather than bland ad-speak (50/50 prior unless you change it). score is the raw cosine against the axis. sense is P(this reads as a message, not nonsense). rel is cosine to the brief. PASS means p ≥ threshold and nothing disqualifies the line. Read the limitations below before trusting a number.

What this is, and is not

  • It ranks stylistic proximity to classic taglines. It is a triage signal for hundreds of candidates, not a judge of a single line, and never legal clearance.
  • The browser build uses a small English model (…). It is weaker than the Python package's default (multilingual-e5-large): on the held-out set it still separates famous lines from bland ad-speak well, but it is more easily fooled by on-topic product descriptions and by fluent junk. Numbers are in the repository's docs/results.md.
  • Failing is weak evidence: most good-but-unknown lines fail. Passing is the useful side.
  • Quotes and proverbs pass. Non-English lines are flagged, not scored reliably.
