Does it read like famous ad copy?

?

The grade says how close a line sits to famous taglines, on seven steps: Excellent · Great · Good · Fair · Average · Weak · Flat. Excellent and Great are where famous lines land; Flat is where product descriptions land. The ring is the calibrated chance the line is great copy rather than bland ad-speak; above the threshold (50% by default) it passes.

  • Green: Good or better. Amber: Fair / Average. Red: Weak / Flat.
  • Gray, “Out”: disqualified before grading. Tap the line to see why (copies a famous line, not a message, placeholder, chat-bot text, too long).
  • Add a brief and lines that drift off topic lose their place in the ranking.
  • Tap any line for the raw numbers: cosine score, sense (does it read as a message), relevance to the brief.

Scope: tagline-length lines, in English. Failing is weak evidence (most good-but-unknown lines fail); passing is the useful signal. Quotes and proverbs pass. This browser build runs a small model; the Python package with the full model is stricter about on-topic product descriptions. Details and numbers: docs/results.md.

Privacy: your lines are embedded on your device and never sent anywhere. The page loads its code from cdn.jsdelivr.net and the model once from huggingface.co.
